## Title

ExportAgent: an AI export desk for apparel exporters | NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory

## Description

ExportAgent reads every buyer email and purchase order the way a careful merchandiser would. It extracts the PO, finds what changed against the previous version, checks whether the factory can still make it, drafts the reply, and produces the commercial invoice and packing list, with a person approving every step. In this demo, a German buyer quietly raises the quantity by 12.5% and pulls the ex-factory date forward. Nemotron Nano reads the email, Python finds the plan is 4,186 pieces short, so the risk check escalates from Nemotron Super to Nemotron Ultra. The fact-checked reply offers two delivery options computed in Python, and the same order is compared across factories in India and Bangladesh. All numbers are computed in Python, never by a model.
